Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains what personal data D2 Infomakers collects, why, who receives it and what choices you have. It applies to our apps, to Gelatino and to this website.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is S GARCIA DE GOES NETO NEGOCIOS DIGITAIS LTDA, CNPJ 52.773.851/0001-70, Av. Nove de Julho, 873, Jardim Apolo I, São José dos Campos – SP, 12243-000, Brazil, which operates the D2 Infomakers brand. For any privacy question or request, write to contact@d2infomakers.site.

2. What we collect

  • Account and purchase data: name, e-mail address, country, products bought, order numbers, amounts and payment status. We receive this from our checkout and payment partners. We never receive or store your full card number.
  • What you enter in the app: your answers to the initial questionnaire (such as your goal and your body weight), your daily check-ins and your progress.
  • Your conversations with Gelatino: the questions, photos or audio you send and the answers you receive.
  • Usage and technical data: features used, device and browser type, IP address and error logs, collected to run, protect and improve the app.
  • Support messages: what you write to us and our replies.

3. How we use it

  • to give you access to the app and to Gelatino;
  • to process payments, renewals, cancellations and refunds;
  • to answer your messages and send service e-mails, such as access links, receipts and billing notices;
  • to prevent fraud and abuse and to keep the service secure;
  • to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations;
  • to improve our products using aggregated statistics.

We may also send you e-mails about our products. You can opt out at any time using the link in the message or by writing to us.

4. Legal bases

We process personal data to perform our contract with you, to comply with legal obligations, for our legitimate interests (such as security and improving the app) and, where the law requires it, with your consent. Health-related information you choose to enter, such as your weight, is used only to run the app for you and only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

5. Who receives your data

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business and that must protect it:

  • payment processing and checkout (Stripe and KashPay);
  • online resellers through which the protocol app is sold;
  • hosting, database and app infrastructure providers;
  • e-mail delivery providers;
  • artificial intelligence providers that generate Gelatino's answers;
  • public authorities, when the law requires it.

6. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. We work with providers that adopt appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

7. How long we keep it

We keep personal data while your account is active and for as long as needed for the purposes above, including legal, tax and accounting obligations. When it is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under Brazil's LGPD, the EU or UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, to object to or restrict certain uses and to withdraw your consent. To exercise these rights, write to contact@d2infomakers.site from the e-mail linked to your account. You may also file a complaint with your data protection authority; in Brazil, the ANPD.

9. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), access is limited to people who need it, and card details are handled only by our payment partners.

10. Children

Our products are intended for adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. This website

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It only stores your language choice in your browser. Our hosting and security providers may process technical data, such as your IP address, to deliver and protect the site.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version, and important changes will be announced by e-mail or in the app. Questions: contact@d2infomakers.site.
